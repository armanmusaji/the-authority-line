WHAT IT IS
A single-page, deterministic simulation. One fictional brand (Halfmoon Tea), one queued email campaign, one incident (an expired offer code found at 2:14 AM), one permission with three positions: Act alone, Ask first, Flag only. The viewer picks a position, replays the night step by step, and sees a "While you were away" return screen that differs per position. A locked row states the agent may never create or change a discount. No backend, no LLM calls, no routing, no auth.
